Résumé

About me

Curious about the questions data can answer.

I came to analytics through economics — interested less in the theory and more in what happens when you test it against real data.

I have a background in economics and a Master's in Business Analytics. The thread between the two is the same: I like understanding why people and systems behave the way they do, and building tools that turn that understanding into something useful.

What I work on

Most of my work sits at the intersection of a few things:

  • Understanding user and customer behavior — segmentation, churn, engagement, and the patterns underneath them.
  • Predictive analytics and machine learning — building models that hold up outside the notebook.
  • Financial modeling — valuation, risk, and revenue analysis where the numbers have to be right.
  • Applied AI systems — multi-agent and retrieval systems that do real work, not demos.
  • Building usable analytical products — dashboards and interfaces that make analysis accessible.

Beyond that, I have a genuine interest in sports analytics and biomechanics — partly from my own years as a Division I athlete, and partly because movement is a hard, satisfying data problem.
